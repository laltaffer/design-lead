# Taste is infrastructure

AI design output converges. Ask any model for "a premium landing page" often enough
and you will meet the same cream canvas, the same safe serif, the same three feature
cards, the same numbered section markers — across projects, across briefs, across
months. The cause is not your prompt and not your tools. It's model priors: absent
stronger forces, generation falls back to the distribution's center, and the center
is slop.

The industry's two answers both help and both stall:

- **Prompt payloads** (design skills, style packs) push one output off the center.
  But they're stateless — the hundredth run knows nothing the first didn't.
- **Linters** catch "reads as generated." But a floor is not a ceiling — no detector
  knows your world, your objective, or what you shipped last quarter.

The missing layer is **memory with authority**: a system that records what your
portfolio actually shipped, what your model's own hands keep reaching for, and what
your corrections were — verbatim — and then consults that record before pixels, every
time. That's what this pack is. Not another player; the referee and the memory.

Its load-bearing rules were each paid for on real work:

**World before pixels.** Every direction that lands is anchored to a named visual
tradition with its own rules. Slop appears where there is no world and the model falls
back to priors.

**Replace, never delete.** Tells are common because they work. A round of comps
mechanically stripped of every tell came back unanimously worse. The tells list is
lint, not a brief: commit to the strongest positive move, then swap flagged tells for
stronger ones.

**Contemporary web is the core diet; analog is flavor.** Boards built analog-first
produce museum pastiche. Award-tier live web supplies structure and personality;
archives supply texture.

**Fresh eyes.** Reference screenshots are consumables — re-harvest every run, keep
only the steal-note text. An eye that recycles one harvest stops learning.

**The steal must transfer the job.** Every structure does a job for its own product.
Name the job before borrowing the structure; check your page shares it.

**Evidence before judgment.** Reviews lead with deterministic detector output, then
opinion labeled as opinion. Blended reviews decay into vibes.

**Results over mandates.** No standing tool bans, no standing tool worship. Contested
tools run the same real brief; outcomes decide; the verdict gets re-examined as tools
change.

**Corrections compound or they're wasted.** A rejection captured verbatim, with its
why, in one findable home, makes the next project start smarter. A correction merely
obeyed is re-learned forever.

Every rule here is a check a human can veto. The registries call out; the human
decides. That's the difference between taste infrastructure and taste automation —
the system remembers so that judgment, not memory, is what the human spends.
